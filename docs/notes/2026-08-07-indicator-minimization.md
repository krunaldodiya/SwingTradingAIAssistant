# Indicator minimization constraint

Status: **accepted operating constraint**

The research tool should not depend on technical indicators by default. The
preferred evidence is directly observable, explainable price action, market
structure, liquidity, volume, relative strength, event, and risk facts.

An indicator is allowed only through an approved specification that proves it
is mandatory for a defined decision, freezes its formula/version and
point-in-time inputs, and validates why simpler facts are insufficient. This
limits redundancy, overfitting, and opaque reasoning while preserving a narrow
path for a demonstrably necessary calculation. The consuming AI never computes
indicators independently.
