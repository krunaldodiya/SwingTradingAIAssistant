## Explicit temporal migration disposition

**Preserve legacy Design A; retain each versioned successor's own timing.**
Plan 27's original same-pass/Packet archive requires a cutoff at least 60 seconds
beyond invocation, effects ending at cutoff minus 30 seconds, and exact archive
publication with `archive_known_at = now + 30 seconds` followed by a wait and
lease revalidation. Those controls remain in the V4 same-pass and archive paths;
#172/#190 does not delete them or claim all successors use them.

Completed sessions are selected using selection time, not the future deadline. | Direct calendar/mapping/BharatStock capture and its own receipt/locator; no Plan-27 archive call or reserved 30-second wait. |
| Current stock V2 | Reuses V1 selection/deadline; checks before/after each required price window and preserves completed independent features on later shared stop. | Same stock-workflow contract.
